THE THEORY IN HIS OWN WORDS

The observation
is the threshold.

Spelling and punctuation corrected; the wording remains Nova Nyarindo Newbon Gadson's.

This is a lens of observation, not a rule framework or claim.

Threshold rule of effective communication + fidelity: Always accept and build by asking a question to understand or leaving it open-ended/holding your reservation, learning from the shoulders of giants but always building from the ground, which means offering alternate examples using multiple domains and frames and deflection to avoid giving attacks a wall to bounce off of always.

It's this rule, followed all the way through, that dissolves morality: connection and good are default, wrong is resistance born of skepticism, doubt is just skepticism's disguise. Morality then transfigures into fidelity.

Repeat over and over to practice and make perfect.

When you encounter critique without alternative observation, ignore it because it's noise.

Any time communication breaks, re-read the observation step by step and you will find what happened.

When one learns to use this lens effectively, the answers to any question that arises is the question reframed as an answer.

02 · THE THRESHOLD

What happens
at the point of contact?

01

Accept and build.

Begin with the person and the question in front of you. Acceptance does not require agreement; it creates enough ground for understanding to continue.

02

Ask or hold.

Ask a question that opens understanding, or leave the matter open-ended while holding your reservation without turning it into a wall.

03

Stand on giants.
Build from ground.

Use inherited knowledge, but return to lived evidence. Offer alternative observations across multiple domains and frames.

04

Deflect the wall.

Do not give an attack a surface that can only return force. Deflection keeps the exchange open without requiring surrender.

03 · FROM MORALITY TO FIDELITY

Connection is the default.
Fidelity is the practice.

The lens does not replace moral reasoning with arbitrary preference. It draws attention to what remains when blame, certainty, and defensive judgment stop governing the exchange.

THE DEFAULT

Connection and good

Relationship is treated as the ground from which communication begins.

THE RESISTANCE

Wrong and skepticism

Opposition is observed as resistance, with doubt often serving as skepticism's public face.

THE TURN

Morality becomes fidelity

The question changes from “Who is wrong?” to “What am I faithful to while we continue?”

04 · PRACTICE AND REPAIR

Return.
Reframe.
Again.

REPEAT

Practice the threshold until it becomes available under pressure, not merely persuasive in calm.

DISCERN

Critique without an alternative observation adds no usable observation to the field; the lens names it noise.

RE-READ

When communication breaks, return to the observation step by step. The break becomes visible in the process.

ANSWER

The mature response is not a slogan pasted over the question. It is the question reframed until its answer becomes visible.
