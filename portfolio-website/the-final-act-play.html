BEFORE THE CURTAIN

A symbolic court, not a verdict on any person.

Everyone shown—including religious figures, public figures, fictional characters, institutions, nations, systems, and the author—is used here as an archetype. Inclusion is symbolic. It is not a factual claim about any person, a declaration on behalf of any tradition, or an endorsement of any system.

The play distinguishes observation from interpretation, and interpretation from the rules we attach to either. It does not ask the audience to confuse a character with a person or a critique with a complete diagnosis.

Production and performance principles

The production keeps the approved screenplay as its structural source. Recurring characters retain a recognisable physical identity across plays; costume, grooming, props, and posture may express a new archetypal role.

For a final voiced master, every speaking role requires a distinct performance consistent with its identity, apparent age, and gender presentation. Real, willing, credited performers are preferred where lawful, feasible, affordable, and technically suitable. Synthetic voices are previsualisation, timing, and fallback tools. Voice or likeness cloning requires explicit consent.

Current release gates include copyright, censorship, likeness, and performer-contract clearance. This browser play is an interactive reading and previsualisation surface.
