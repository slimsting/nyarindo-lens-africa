THE BENCH IS NOT A COMPROMISE

Neither has to disappear for the other to be present.

Science and religion are not interchangeable. Science tests public claims about the world. Religion shapes practices, communities, meanings, and commitments. The meeting I care about is not a blurring of those differences, but a shared willingness to remain honest in their presence.

TWO DISCIPLINES OF HONESTY

01 · CURIOSITY

Science at its best

Looks closely. Says what can be tested and what cannot yet be concluded. Changes the model when evidence asks it to. Treats uncertainty as an invitation to investigate, not a failure to conceal.

02 · FAITH

Faith at its best

Holds what matters while knowledge is incomplete. Forms the courage to live before every answer arrives. Remains open to correction, avoids confusing certainty with virtue, and remembers that a mystery is not a licence for contempt.

THE GREEN BETWEEN FAITH AND SCIENCE

The meeting point is attention.

Attention asks me to look before I defend, listen before I label, and make room to look again. It is the practice that keeps curiosity from becoming arrogance and faith from becoming blindness.

A WORKING AGREEMENT

Keep both hats honest.

  1. 01

    Do not make certainty a costume.

    Neither evidence nor belief becomes stronger by pretending it has no limits.

  2. 02

    Let curiosity include reverence.

    To study something closely can be a form of respect rather than an act of reduction.

  3. 03

    Return to attention.

    The shared discipline is to notice what is present, remain teachable, and keep the question open long enough to learn.
