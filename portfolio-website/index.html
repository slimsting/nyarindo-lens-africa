01 LISTENING

Drift.

PROTOTYPE

What if finding music felt like following a friend’s trail? Drift explores a quieter kind of discovery: no ads, no paid placement, just songs moving through people and possibility.

Step into Drift ↗
A SOFTER CURRENT
Project explanation · design and Lens

Design process

Begin with the listener’s intended direction: stay, explore, transform, contrast, or surprise. Make each recommendation explain its path, so discovery feels like a human trail rather than a black box.

Through the Lens

A listening mood is a container; the pulse can expand into unfamiliar music or contract toward the familiar. The prototype makes that choice visible. Its sample tracks and tones are placeholders, not a connected music catalogue.

02 WELLBEING

IN FORMATION

A gentle space to notice what is happening, keep the context around it, and return with a little more understanding. The teachings become a practice, not a lecture.

Project explanation · design and Lens

Design process

Begin with attention, then separate what was observed from what it might mean. Keep the surrounding context beside the memory, and leave room for one small application. Returning matters more than building a streak.

Through the Lens

The person is not reduced to a label. A moment is held with its context, and its meaning remains open to revision. The early practice stays on this device and offers reflection prompts; it does not diagnose or prescribe.

Design process

The experiment grows from Book Seven’s vacuum simulation: particles move under simple attraction and repulsion, so clusters can emerge without being assigned. The semantic prototype gives information nodes vectors, then lets the field negotiate their positions.

Through the Lens

Structure is explored as something that can emerge through relationship. The later Semantic Field OS specification extends this idea into a retrieval architecture; this browser experiment demonstrates only an earlier, smaller part of that direction.

SEVEN BOOKS · ONE RETURNING QUESTION

A spiral,
not a staircase.

The books are not sequels marching toward a final answer. They revisit familiar human challenges—attention, relationships, learning, meaning, and systems—from another angle and at a deeper level. The same ground returns; we meet it with more context, more play, and different questions.

☼

SCIENCE · RELIGION · THE SPACE BETWEEN

Nyarindo wearing
both Hats

I do not experience science and religion as rival rooms. They are two disciplines of attention: one follows evidence into the unknown; the other holds faith while questions remain open. Each becomes more honest when it refuses to impersonate the other.

01 · CURIOSITY

Follow what can be tested.

Science asks us to look closely, name uncertainty, test the claim, and revise the story when the evidence changes.

02 · FAITH

Remain faithful while learning.

Faith is not the refusal to ask questions. It can be the devotion that keeps us present while the answer is still unfolding.

Where they meet is attention—the practice of looking before certainty, staying with what is here, and making room to look again.

AN OFFERING · A WORKING THEORY

The chase for coherence.

I do not think meaning is a perfect answer waiting to be found. I think it is the continuing chase: the movement toward a life whose attention, understanding, and action can belong together.

01 · MEANING

The chase

Meaning is not a static possession. It is the ongoing work of bringing what we notice, what we believe, and how we live into a truer relationship.

02 · THE DRIFT

Incoherence

Much of what we call entropy, stress, anxiety, and human trouble can be understood through incoherence: the felt separation between reality, story, feeling, and response.

03 · THE ROOT

Poor attention

When attention is scattered, captive, or dishonest, incoherence grows. To return is to notice what we are actually meeting, how we are meeting it, and what we have stopped seeing.

A note on definitions: These are not dictionary-accurate claims. They are working tools I use—as carefully as I can—to explain reality and make the path toward coherence visible.
