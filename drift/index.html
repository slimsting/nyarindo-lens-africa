    THE SHAPE OF DRIFT

    How Drift works.

    Public, private, and shared are different containers

    Public Drift is the discovery layer. Your Playground is local and private. A Shared Room is an explicit, invite-only version of a story you choose to share.

    Where does the music come from?

    Official source links and embeds. Drift does not host or download public media, and a source can disappear or disable embedding.

    What does Following mean?

    It means you accept updates from that artist. It does not change recommendations, expose your history, or change the public experience.

    Why can a source be unavailable?

    A creator or platform may remove, restrict, or block it. The playlist and personal story can remain while official playback becomes unavailable.

    What lives in the Playground?

    Your albums, playlists, imported links, notes, lyric timing, themes, and optional local media or font files. Nothing is uploaded by default.

    What happens to my data?

    It stays in this browser. You can export Markdown or JSON and clear it locally at any time.

    OWNER CONTROL LANE

    Allow. Restrict. Block.

    1. ClaimIdentify the source and the rights you represent.
    2. VerifyConfirm control through the provider account, official domain, or authorised representative.
    3. Negotiate privatelyDrift receives only the final permission state.
    4. ApplyControl embed, lyrics, video, catalogue, and future sharing separately.

    Verified disputes do not automatically erase someone’s private local story. Public availability and further sharing follow the agreement.
